The annual Costa Well Grown Community Grants Program for the Armidale and Guyra region is now open for applications. This vital program invests in local projects that strengthen the social fabric and economic resilience of our agricultural heartlands through education and health support.

Premium Citrus Harvesting

Our citrus orchards are managed with absolute precision to meet global quality standards. Every fruit is hand-picked at the peak of ripeness to ensure a rich vitamin C profile and natural sweetness. We use sustainable farming techniques to protect the soil health for future generations of Australian growers. Our technology-led approach ensures that only the most vibrant and juicy citrus fruits reach your table every single day.

World-Class Grapes & Vineyards

Costa's vineyards are situated in prime climatic regions that allow for the production of superior table grapes. We monitor moisture and nutrient levels daily to ensure large, sweet bunches that offer a crisp texture and deep flavor. Our trellis systems protect the delicate clusters from environmental stress while maximizing sunlight exposure. We take immense pride in delivering grapes that represent the pinnacle of agricultural innovation and quality control.

Succulent Strawberry Production

Our strawberries are grown in controlled environments using advanced substrate technology to minimize water waste and soil diseases. We prioritize varieties known for their deep red color, firm texture, and incredible natural aroma. By utilizing greenhouse farming, we can provide a consistent supply of premium strawberries year-round. Every berry is carefully inspected for size and sweetness, ensuring a perfect snacking experience in every punnet we ship.

Advanced Glasshouse Tomatoes

Our glasshouse tomato facilities are a marvel of modern agricultural engineering, producing high-density nutrition year-round. Using hydroponic systems, we deliver precise nutrients directly to the roots, resulting in superior flavor and vibrant color. We rely on biological controls instead of harsh chemicals to maintain a healthy ecosystem within our greenhouses. Whether it is snacking tomatoes or large trusses, we focus on culinary versatility and consistent quality.

Agricultural Infrastructure Future

We are building the future of food security through massive investments in solar energy and water recycling plants across our farms. This infrastructure allows us to achieve high yields on a smaller footprint, preserving natural habitats around our facilities. Our focus on stewardship means we are feeding a growing population while protecting the planet's vital resources. We integrate data analytics to optimize harvest cycles, reducing waste and ensuring maximum efficiency in the supply chain.

Grown around the world

We fly high the flag of Australian-grown quality, working sustainably with the land to maximise optimal growing conditions around the country. Expanding our expertise globally, we've developed berry farms in China, Morocco and Laos.
